# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-04-27

Bebito is designed to be private by default. This policy explains, in plain language, what happens to the information you enter into the app.

## What we collect

Nothing. All entries — activities, milk feeds, weights and the optional baby profile — are saved only in this device's local storage.

We do not operate any servers that receive your data, and we do not require you to create an account.

## Third parties and tracking

The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party SDKs that transmit personal data.

No cookies are used.

## Backups

Backups, including JSON or CSV files, are generated on your device.

Where you save or share them is entirely under your control.

## Children's data

The app is intended for caregivers tracking infant routines.

Because nothing leaves your device, no third party — including us — ever receives data about you or your child.

## Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at support@example.com.
